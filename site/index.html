Own-your-hardware AI.

MINTI turns the machines you already have into a private AI mesh — open agents, open weights, no cloud, no accounts, nothing leaves your LAN. Install it on your daily machine, or boot an old laptop back to life as a dedicated node. Your browser is the whole interface.

How it works

Every machine's browser opens its own workspace; the Clan routes work to whoever is strongest.

Install in three steps

Windows
  1. Extract the zip.
  2. Double-click Install-MINTI.cmd and accept the UAC prompt.
  3. Your browser opens the workspace at http://127.0.0.1:8088.
Linux
  1. tar xzf minti-linux-amd64-v0.4.0-D1.tar.gz
  2. cd minti-linux-amd64-v0.4.0-D1 && sudo bash install/install.sh
  3. The workspace opens (or visit http://127.0.0.1:8088).
macOS
  1. tar xzf minti-macos-<arch>-v0.4.0-D1.tar.gz
  2. cd into it && sudo bash install-minti.sh
  3. The workspace opens at http://127.0.0.1:8088.

Uninstall anytime — uninstall scripts ship in the download and preserve your Clan identity by default.

Verify your download first: checksums.txt

Models run through Ollama. The installer detects it, or points you to ollama.com — it never installs third-party software for you. (Linux: the installer offers the official Ollama install script; set MINTI_NO_OLLAMA=1 to skip.)

door A

Revive a machine

live preview — disk installer coming

A lean bootable OS image with the whole MINTI stack preinstalled — made for 10-year-old laptops (1–2 GB RAM). Flash it to a USB stick, boot, and the machine joins your Clan as a node.

The current image is a live preview: it runs entirely from RAM and forgets everything on reboot. The disk installer (wipe & install) is the next milestone. The ISO lands on the releases page when the next image build completes — it is not uploaded yet.

When it's here: flash & boot

  1. Download the .iso and verify its SHA-256.
  2. Write it to a USB stick with balenaEtcher, Rufus, or dd.
  3. Boot the old machine from USB (usually F12 / Esc at power-on).

x86-64, 1–2 GB RAM, any disk. WiFi firmware included.

Known warnings — read before installing

Unsigned binaries (for now)

These builds are not code-signed yet. Windows SmartScreen will say 'Windows protected your PC' — click 'More info', then 'Run anyway'. macOS Gatekeeper may require right-click → Open, or 'Open Anyway' in System Settings > Privacy & Security. ALWAYS verify the SHA-256 against checksums.txt before clicking through a warning — the hash check is your protection.

Windows Defender

Unsigned services doing LAN discovery can trigger Defender's behavioural blocks. The installer prints the two folder exclusions to add if your service stops minutes after starting.

Hardware expectations

The stack itself is light. The models are not: a 3B model wants ~4 GB free RAM, an 8B model ~8–10 GB. On small machines stick to small models — heavy swapping looks like a broken machine and isn't.

One open port

Only the Clan daemon listens on the network (TCP 7777, scoped to private networks on Windows). The runtime and the workspace bind 127.0.0.1 and are unreachable from the LAN.
