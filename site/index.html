MINTI

Open agents. Open weights. Clan-aware.

Old laptops, joined into one trust group, with no server in the middle.

A bootable Debian desktop you flash onto a machine that is otherwise on its way to being e-waste. Each one is given a name from the sky, finds the others on the network, proves who it is, and passes work to whichever machine can actually do it. A 4 GB laptop with no models on it can ask a question and have the strongest machine in the house answer. Nothing leaves for a company; there is no account and no cloud endpoint in the path.

It is a research vehicle first, and the question is narrow enough to fail: can a household's old machines form a trust group with no server, and can the weakest of them earn a real role in it?
